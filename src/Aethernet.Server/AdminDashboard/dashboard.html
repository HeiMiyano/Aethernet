Where do I find these?

Your UID is shown in the plugin's main window (/aethernet). Your secret key is the SecretKey value in %APPDATA%\XIVLauncher\pluginConfigs\Aethernet.json on the PC you play on. The account must be linked to Discord and have the admin or moderator role.
